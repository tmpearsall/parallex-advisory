**Three ways to work together**
1. **Technical diligence.** A fixed-scope review of a company's technology, data and AI claims, written so a family can read it and decide. Delivered in about two weeks. [CONFIRM turnaround]
2. **Advisor on call.** A monthly retainer: one technical voice you can phone before a decision, who already knows your deals. [CONFIRM price; plan range was $5,000 to $15,000 a month]
3. **Fractional CTO.** Hands-on leadership inside the company after close, with a written plan to hand off to a full-time hire. [CONFIRM terms]

**Why a family office.** Many family offices back operating businesses that need technology brought in, and have no technical staff of their own. The sponsor's demo is often the only technical review the deal gets. That is the gap.

**What you get.** A document you can hand to a partner, a sibling, a board or a lender. It says what is real, what is a demo, what breaks at scale, what it costs to fix, and what to ask the sponsor.
